# Cross-Tradition Resonance Log

Use this log to prevent the corpus from leaning on the same parallels too often. A resonance is ready for publication only when it names a specific passage, a structural homology, and a productive divergence.

## High-Frequency Anchors

These are useful but at risk of overuse:

- **Bhagavad Gita 2.47 / 2.48-50** — non-possessive action, skill in action, equanimity under outcome uncertainty.
- **Epictetus, Enchiridion 1** — governance of impressions, what is up to us, pre-reactive freedom.
- **Yoga Sutra 1.2-1.3 / 3.1-3.3** — settling fluctuations, attention refinement, samyama.
- **Vijñana Bhairava Tantra breath and sound yuktis** — threshold awareness, direct contemplative triggers.
- **Dao De Jing 1 / 16 / 34** — apophasis, stillness, non-possessive source activity.

## Underused Anchors To Rotate In

- **Kena Upanishad 1.3-1.9** — faculties cannot objectify their own ground.
- **Mundaka Upanishad 1.1.4-1.1.5 / 2.2.8-2.2.9** — higher and lower knowledge; knowledge that cuts ignorance.
- **Katha Upanishad 1.2.18-25** — death as pedagogical threshold and discernment of the imperishable.
- **Plotinus, Enneads I.6 / V.1 / VI.9** — ascent, emanation, return to the One.
- **Zhuangzi, Cook Ding / butterfly / heart-fasting** — non-forcing, transformation, emptied receptivity.
- **Phaedo 95a-102a** — cause versus condition, Forms as explanatory principles.
- **Pratyabhijnahrdayam 1-6 / 20** — recognition of citi, contraction, grace and effort.
- **Siva Sutra I.1-I.5 / I.12** — consciousness as Self, knowledge as bondage, udyama, wonder.

## Current Corpus Notes

- `Zhuangzi` and `Phaedo` already model strong divergence clauses.
- `Vijñana Bhairava Tantra` needs the most resonance cleanup from Yukti 38 onward: repeated Stoic/Yoga Sutra/phenomenology parallels should be replaced with verse-specific anchors.
- `Bhagavad Gita` uses resonances well but should diversify beyond Stoicism, VBT, and Upanishadic parallels in later passes.

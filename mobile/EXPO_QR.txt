If Expo Go shows:
  "Project is incompatible with this version of Expo Go.
   The project you requested requires a newer version of Expo Go."

Your phone’s Expo Go is OLDER than SDK 54. You need Expo Go iOS **54.0.0**
(App Store → Expo Go → Update, or delete & reinstall). Check version in
Expo Go → Profile (must match 54.x for this repo).

  curl http://192.168.1.128:8000/health
  curl http://192.168.1.128:8082/status

     (or scan QR from that terminal)

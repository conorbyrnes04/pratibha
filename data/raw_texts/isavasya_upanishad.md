## Fullness and the Non-Diminishing Whole
**Source:** Invocation (Purnamada)

ॐ पूर्णमदः पूर्णमिदं पूर्णात्पूर्णमुदच्यते ।  
पूर्णस्य पूर्णमादाय पूर्णमेवावशिष्यते ॥

### Devanagari
ॐ पूर्णमदः पूर्णमिदं पूर्णात्पूर्णमुदच्यते ।  
पूर्णस्य पूर्णमादाय पूर्णमेवावशिष्यते ॥

### IAST
om purnam adah purnam idam purnat purnam udacyate |  
purnasya purnam adaya purnam eva avasisyate ||

### Pratibha Translation
That is fullness; this is fullness. From fullness, fullness appears.  
When fullness is taken from fullness, fullness alone remains.

### Pratibha Commentary
The invocation establishes the metaphysical grammar for the entire text: reality is not a scarce container but an inexhaustible whole. Manifestation does not reduce source. This dissolves possessiveness before verse one begins.

### Key Terms
**purnam (पूर्णम्)** - fullness, completeness, non-lack.

### Cross-Tradition Resonances
**Neoplatonic procession:** emergence without depletion.

### Practice (Abhyasa)
Repeat this invocation once before study and notice one place where your mind is operating from lack.

## Renunciation in Action
**Source:** Isavasya Upanishad 1-2

ईशावास्यमिदं सर्वं यत्किञ्च जगत्यां जगत् ।  
तेन त्यक्तेन भुञ्जीथा मा गृधः कस्यस्विद्धनम् ॥ १ ॥

कुर्वन्नेवेह कर्माणि जिजीविषेच्छतं समाः ।  
एवं त्वयि नान्यथेतोऽस्ति न कर्म लिप्यते नरे ॥ २ ॥

### Devanagari
ईशावास्यमिदं सर्वं यत्किञ्च जगत्यां जगत् ।  
तेन त्यक्तेन भुञ्जीथा मा गृधः कस्यस्विद्धनम् ॥ १ ॥

कुर्वन्नेवेह कर्माणि जिजीविषेच्छतं समाः ।  
एवं त्वयि नान्यथेतोऽस्ति न कर्म लिप्यते नरे ॥ २ ॥

### IAST
isavasyam idam sarvam yat kinca jagatyam jagat |  
tena tyaktena bhunjitha ma grdah kasya svid dhanam || 1 ||

kurvanneveha karmani jijivisec chatam samah |  
evam tvayi nanyatheto'sti na karma lipyate nare || 2 ||

### Pratibha Translation
All this moving world is pervaded by the Lord. Through renunciation, enjoy; do not covet what belongs to another.

One may wish to live a full life doing action here - only in this way does action cease to bind.

### Pratibha Commentary
The text refuses the split between world-rejection and worldliness. Renunciation is not withdrawal from life but non-grasping participation in it. Action remains, ownership loosens. This is the opening synthesis of karma and jnana.

### Key Terms
**isavasyam** - pervaded, clothed, inhabited by the Lord.

**tyaktena** - by relinquishment, by non-grasping.

### Cross-Tradition Resonances
**Bhagavad Gita karma-yoga:** action without attachment.

### Practice (Abhyasa)
Choose one ordinary task today and perform it without inner claim of "mine."

## Self, Speed, Stillness, and Non-Hatred
**Source:** Isavasya Upanishad 3-8

अनेजदेकं मनसो जवीयो नैनद्देवा आप्नुवन्पूर्वमर्षत् ।  
तद्धावतोऽन्यानत्येति तिष्ठत्तस्मिन्नपो मातरिश्वा दधाति ॥ ४ ॥

तदेजति तन्नैजति तद्दूरे तद्वन्तिके ।  
तदन्तरस्य सर्वस्य तदु सर्वस्यास्य बाह्यतः ॥ ५ ॥

यस्तु सर्वाणि भूतान्यात्मन्येवानुपश्यति ।  
सर्वभूतेषु चात्मानं ततो न विजुगुप्सते ॥ ६ ॥

### Devanagari
अनेजदेकं मनसो जवीयो नैनद्देवा आप्नुवन्पूर्वमर्षत् ।  
तद्धावतोऽन्यानत्येति तिष्ठत्तस्मिन्नपो मातरिश्वा दधाति ॥ ४ ॥

तदेजति तन्नैजति तद्दूरे तद्वन्तिके ।  
तदन्तरस्य सर्वस्य तदु सर्वस्यास्य बाह्यतः ॥ ५ ॥

यस्तु सर्वाणि भूतान्यात्मन्येवानुपश्यति ।  
सर्वभूतेषु चात्मानं ततो न विजुगुप्सते ॥ ६ ॥

### IAST
anejad ekam manaso javiyo nainad deva apnuvan purvam arsat |  
tad dhavato'nyan atyeti tisthat tasminn apo matarisva dadhati || 4 ||

tad ejati tan naijati tad dure tad v antike |  
tad antar asya sarvasya tadu sarvasyasya bahyatah || 5 ||

yas tu sarvani bhutany atmany evanupasyati |  
sarvabhutesu catmanam tato na vijugupsate || 6 ||

### Pratibha Translation
The Self is unmoving yet swifter than the mind; senses cannot overtake it. It moves and does not move, far and near, within all and outside all.

Who sees all beings in the Self and the Self in all beings no longer recoils in hatred.

### Pratibha Commentary
These paradoxes are not poetic confusion; they dismantle object-based thinking. The Self cannot be grasped as one finite thing among others. Realization is tested ethically: non-dual vision ends hatred, not just speculation.

### Key Terms
**anupasyati** - sees continuously, not as a one-time concept.

**ekatva** - unity, non-separation.

### Cross-Tradition Resonances
**Kena and Katha Upanishads:** non-objectifiable Self.

### Practice (Abhyasa)
In one conflict, pause and ask: "What am I refusing to include in my sense of self?"

## Vidya and Avidya Together
**Source:** Isavasya Upanishad 9-11

अन्धन्तमः प्रविशन्ति येऽविद्यामुपासते ।  
ततो भूय इव ते तमो य उ विद्यायां रताः ॥ ९ ॥

विद्यां चाविद्यां च यस्तद्वेदोभयं सह ।  
अविद्यया मृत्युं तीर्त्वा विद्ययामृतमश्नुते ॥ ११ ॥

### Devanagari
अन्धन्तमः प्रविशन्ति येऽविद्यामुपासते ।  
ततो भूय इव ते तमो य उ विद्यायां रताः ॥ ९ ॥

विद्यां चाविद्यां च यस्तद्वेदोभयं सह ।  
अविद्यया मृत्युं तीर्त्वा विद्ययामृतमश्नुते ॥ ११ ॥

### IAST
andham tamah pravisanti ye'vidyam upasate |  
tato bhuya iva te tamo ya u vidyayam ratah || 9 ||

vidyam cavidyam ca yas tad vedobhayam saha |  
avidyaya mrtyum tirtva vidyayamrtam asnute || 11 ||

### Pratibha Translation
Those devoted only to ignorance enter darkness; those devoted only to knowledge enter deeper darkness.  
One who knows both together crosses death through avidya and attains immortality through vidya.

### Pratibha Commentary
The Upanishad rejects one-sided absolutism. Pure activism without insight binds. Pure knowledge-talk without embodied duty also binds. Integration is the discipline: practice in the world with insight into the Real.

### Key Terms
**avidya** - pragmatic, world-engaged knowing at the empirical level (also ignorance in other contexts).

**vidya** - liberating knowledge of the Self.

### Cross-Tradition Resonances
**Madhyamaka middle logic:** avoid extremes.

### Practice (Abhyasa)
Balance one contemplative practice with one concrete act of service today.

## Sambhuti and Asambhuti
**Source:** Isavasya Upanishad 12-14

अन्धं तमः प्रविशन्ति येऽसम्भूतिमुपासते ।  
ततो भूय इव ते तमो य उ सम्भूत्यां रताः ॥ १२ ॥

सम्भूतिं च विनाशं च यस्तद्वेदोभयं सह ।  
विनाशेन मृत्युं तीर्त्वा सम्भूत्यामृतमश्नुते ॥ १४ ॥

### Devanagari
अन्धं तमः प्रविशन्ति येऽसम्भूतिमुपासते ।  
ततो भूय इव ते तमो य उ सम्भूत्यां रताः ॥ १२ ॥

सम्भूतिं च विनाशं च यस्तद्वेदोभयं सह ।  
विनाशेन मृत्युं तीर्त्वा सम्भूत्यामृतमश्नुते ॥ १४ ॥

### IAST
andham tamah pravisanti ye'sambhutim upasate |  
tato bhuya iva te tamo ya u sambhutyam ratah || 12 ||

sambhutim ca vinasam ca yas tad vedobhayam saha |  
vinasena mrtyum tirtva sambhutyamrtam asnute || 14 ||

### Pratibha Translation
Exclusive fixation on the unmanifest leads to darkness; exclusive fixation on manifest becoming also leads to darkness.  
Knowing both together, one crosses mortality and realizes immortality.

### Pratibha Commentary
Again the text blocks reductionism. Reality is not exhausted by either metaphysical abstraction or empirical manifestation. The seeker must learn double vision: respect for becoming, rootedness in the unconditioned.

### Key Terms
**sambhuti** - becoming/manifestation.

**asambhuti** - unmanifest source.

### Cross-Tradition Resonances
**Plotinus One and emanation:** source and manifestation without dual rupture.

### Practice (Abhyasa)
In meditation, alternate 2 minutes open awareness (unmanifest) and 2 minutes sensory detail (manifest), then integrate.

## Golden Lid and Final Prayers
**Source:** Isavasya Upanishad 15-18

हिरण्मयेन पात्रेण सत्यस्यापिहितं मुखम् ।  
तत्त्वं पूषन्नपावृणु सत्यधर्माय दृष्टये ॥ १५ ॥

पूषन्नेकर्षे यम सूर्य प्राजापत्य व्यूह रश्मीन् समूह ।  
... योऽसावसौ पुरुषः सोऽहमस्मि ॥ १६ ॥

अग्ने नय सुपथा राये अस्मान् ... नम उक्तिं विधेम ॥ १८ ॥

### Devanagari
हिरण्मयेन पात्रेण सत्यस्यापिहितं मुखम् ।  
तत्त्वं पूषन्नपावृणु सत्यधर्माय दृष्टये ॥ १५ ॥

पूषन्नेकर्षे यम सूर्य प्राजापत्य व्यूह रश्मीन् समूह ।  
तेजो यत्ते रूपं कल्याणतमं तत्ते पश्यामि योऽसावसौ पुरुषः सोऽहमस्मि ॥ १६ ॥

अग्ने नय सुपथा राये अस्मान् विश्वानि देव वयुनानि विद्वान् ।  
युयोध्यस्मज्जुहुराणमेनो भूयिष्ठां ते नम उक्तिं विधेम ॥ १८ ॥

### IAST
hiranmayena patrena satyasyapihitam mukham |  
tat tvam pusan apavrnu satyadharmaya drstaye || 15 ||

pusann ekarse yama surya prajapatya vyuha rasmin samuha |  
tejo yat te rupam kalyanatamam tat te pasyami yo'sav asau purusah so'ham asmi || 16 ||

agne naya supatha raye asman visvani deva vayunani vidvan |  
yuyodhy asmaj juhuranam eno bhuyistham te nama uktim vidhema || 18 ||

### Pratibha Translation
The face of truth is covered by a golden lid - O Sun, remove it for the one devoted to truth.

Gather your rays; let me behold your most auspicious form. That Person there - I am He.

O Fire, lead us by the good path, remove crooked sin from us; to you we offer reverent words.

### Pratibha Commentary
The text closes with prayer, not abstraction. Philosophical insight matures as surrender, remembrance, and guidance at the threshold of death. "So'ham asmi" is not ego-assertion but identity with the deepest Person beyond individuality.

### Key Terms
**hiranmayena patrena** - golden vessel/lid veiling truth.

**so'ham asmi** - "He am I," identity in realization.

### Cross-Tradition Resonances
**Mundaka luminosity verses:** outer light as veil and pointer.

### Practice (Abhyasa)
At night, close practice with: "Lead me by the good path." Recall one crooked tendency and one corrective action for tomorrow.

## Mantra 1
**Source:** Isavasya Upanishad 1

ईशावास्यमिदं सर्वं यत्किञ्च जगत्यां जगत् ।  
तेन त्यक्तेन भुञ्जीथा मा गृधः कस्यस्विद्धनम् ॥ १ ॥

### Devanagari
ईशावास्यमिदं सर्वं यत्किञ्च जगत्यां जगत् ।  
तेन त्यक्तेन भुञ्जीथा मा गृधः कस्यस्विद्धनम् ॥ १ ॥

### IAST
isavasyam idam sarvam yat kinca jagatyam jagat |  
tena tyaktena bhunjitha ma grdah kasya svid dhanam || 1 ||

### Pratibha Translation
All this world is pervaded by the Lord. Enjoy through renunciation; do not covet another's wealth.

### Pratibha Commentary
The opening move joins ontology and ethics: if all is pervaded by the Divine, possessiveness is a distortion of reality.

### Key Terms
**isavasyam** - pervaded by the Lord; **tyaktena** - by relinquishment.

### Cross-Tradition Resonances
Bhagavad Gita karma-yoga non-attachment.

### Practice (Abhyasa)
Do one task today without internally claiming ownership of its result.

## Mantra 3
**Source:** Isavasya Upanishad 3

तांस्ते प्रेत्याभिगच्छन्ति ये के चात्महनो जनाः ॥ ३ ॥

### Practice (Abhyasa)

### Devanagari
स पर्यगाच्छुक्रमकायमव्रणमस्नाविरं शुद्धमपापविद्धम् ।  
कविर्मनीषी परिभूः स्वयंभूर्याथातथ्यतोऽर्थान्व्यदधाच्छाश्वतीभ्यः समाभ्यः ॥ ८ ॥

### IAST
sa paryagac chukram akayam avranam asnaviram suddham apapa-viddham |  
kavir manisi paribhuh svayambhur yathatathyato'rthan vyadadhac chasvatibhyah samabhyah || 8 ||

### Pratibha Translation
All-pervading, pure, bodiless, untouched by evil - wise, transcendent, self-existent, ordering all rightly.

### Pratibha Commentary
The Self is not merely interior witness but cosmically ordering intelligence.

### Key Terms
**svayambhu** - self-existent; **paribhuh** - transcendent.

### Cross-Tradition Resonances
Ishvara descriptions in later Vedanta.

### Practice (Abhyasa)
Before planning, pause and align with "right order," not anxiety-speed.

## Mantra 9
**Source:** Isavasya Upanishad 9

अन्धन्तमः प्रविशन्ति येऽविद्यामुपासते ।  
ततो भूय इव ते तमो य उ विद्यायां रताः ॥ ९ ॥

### Devanagari
अन्धन्तमः प्रविशन्ति येऽविद्यामुपासते ।  
ततो भूय इव ते तमो य उ विद्यायां रताः ॥ ९ ॥

### IAST
andham tamah pravisanti ye'vidyam upasate |  
tato bhuya iva te tamo ya u vidyayam ratah || 9 ||

### Pratibha Translation
Those devoted only to avidya enter darkness; those devoted only to vidya enter deeper darkness.

### Pratibha Commentary
Exclusive one-sidedness - worldly or spiritual - binds.

### Key Terms
**avidya/vidya** - empirical/ultimate knowing modes.

### Cross-Tradition Resonances
Middle-way anti-extremism.

### Practice (Abhyasa)
Balance one practical duty and one contemplative period today.

## Mantra 11
**Source:** Isavasya Upanishad 11

विद्यां चाविद्यां च यस्तद्वेदोभयं सह ।  
अविद्यया मृत्युं तीर्त्वा विद्ययामृतमश्नुते ॥ ११ ॥

### Devanagari
विद्यां चाविद्यां च यस्तद्वेदोभयं सह ।  
अविद्यया मृत्युं तीर्त्वा विद्ययामृतमश्नुते ॥ ११ ॥

### IAST
vidyam cavidyam ca yas tad vedobhayam saha |  
avidyaya mrtyum tirtva vidyayamrtam asnute || 11 ||

### Pratibha Translation
Knowing both together, one crosses death through avidya and attains immortality through vidya.

### Pratibha Commentary
Integration, not exclusion, is the teaching climax of this triad.

### Key Terms
**ubhayam saha** - both together.

### Cross-Tradition Resonances
Karma-jnana synthesis debates.

### Practice (Abhyasa)
Pair your meditation with one concrete ethical action immediately after.

## Mantra 16

तेजो यत्ते रूपं कल्याणतमं तत्ते पश्यामि योऽसावसौ पुरुषः सोऽहमस्मि ॥ १६ ॥

## Mantra 18
**Source:** Isavasya Upanishad 18

अग्ने नय सुपथा राये अस्मान् विश्वानि देव वयुनानि विद्वान् ।  
युयोध्यस्मज्जुहुराणमेनो भूयिष्ठां ते नम उक्तिं विधेम ॥ १८ ॥

### Devanagari
अग्ने नय सुपथा राये अस्मान् विश्वानि देव वयुनानि विद्वान् ।  
युयोध्यस्मज्जुहुराणमेनो भूयिष्ठां ते नम उक्तिं विधेम ॥ १८ ॥

### IAST
agne naya supatha raye asman visvani deva vayunani vidvan |  
yuyodhy asmaj juhuranam eno bhuyistham te nama uktim vidhema || 18 ||

### Pratibha Translation
O Fire, lead us by the good path, knowing all our ways; remove crooked sin from us. We offer reverence.

### Pratibha Commentary
The text ends as ethical prayer: guidance, purification, humility.

### Key Terms
**supatha** - good path; **yuyodhi** - remove, ward off.

### Cross-Tradition Resonances
Purificatory closing prayers across Vedic liturgy.

### Practice (Abhyasa)
Close each day with one sincere request for clearer conduct tomorrow.

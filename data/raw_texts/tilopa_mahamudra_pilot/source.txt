TILOPA — MAHĀMUDRĀ UPADEŚA (The Ganges Mahāmudrā) — PILOT RAW SOURCE
=====================================================================

Text: Tilopa's Mahāmudropadeśa ("Mahāmudrā Instruction"), traditionally given to Nāropa on
  the banks of the Ganges; transmitted in Tibetan (phyag chen gang ma / gang GA ma) in the
  Tengyur, itself from a lost/《reconstructed》 Sanskrit original.
Provenance / license: NO clean single public-domain English translation was located for this
  pilot. The most cited modern renderings (Ken McLeod; Nālandā Translation Committee; Thrangu
  Rinpoche editions) are copyrighted. Therefore the Pratibha Translation below is an ORIGINAL
  editorial English rendering of the well-attested teaching-content of the Ganges Mahāmudrā —
  it does NOT copy any restricted translation.
Language basis: the received text is Tibetan (from Sanskrit). Verified Sanskrit/Devanagari for
  individual lines was NOT sourced for this pilot; the Original layer LABELS the source-language
  basis (Tibetan/Sanskrit) rather than asserting unverified script.

UNIT TIL_001 — Non-Meditation (mahāmudrā cannot be shown)
Teaching-content (well-attested): Mahāmudrā cannot be shown by any symbol; it is not an object
  the mind can grasp. Do not fabricate, do not correct, do not meditate on anything — let mind
  rest in its own uncontrived condition, and the innate wisdom dawns of itself.

UNIT TIL_002 — Mind Like Space
Teaching-content (well-attested): As space cannot be pointed to or bounded, so mind's nature
  has no center or edge. Thoughts arise and dissolve like clouds in a clear sky; do not follow
  them, do not suppress them — recognize the sky that never changes.

UNIT TIL_003 — The Guru's Grace and Self-Recognition
Teaching-content (well-attested): One who exhausts effort and rests in devotion to the guru
  sees the innate. When the guru's pith-instruction strikes the heart, look directly at the
  looker; there is nothing else to find, and self-liberation is complete in that seeing.

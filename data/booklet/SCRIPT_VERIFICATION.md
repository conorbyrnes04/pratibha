**Confidence tags**

- **MEDIUM-HIGH** — the Devanāgarī was produced by transliterating the edition's IAST into Devanāgarī and round-trip checking it (Nāgārjuna's MMK, Śāntideva's Bca). **These especially warrant a scholar's eye**: transliteration can introduce sandhi/segmentation or anusvāra/candrabindu artifacts that round-tripping does not always catch.

**Also flagged inline:** multi-verse selections (Bca), selected-line excerpts (Chāndogya), and any place where the docx *translation* reflects an interpretive rather than literal reading (Spandakārikā sp_01).

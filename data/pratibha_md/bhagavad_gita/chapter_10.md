## Listen Again — Source Beyond Gods and Seers
**Source:** Bhagavad Gītā 10.1–10.3

The Blessed Lord said: Hear again My supreme word, O Mighty-armed, which I speak to you who delight in it, wishing your welfare. Neither the hosts of gods nor the great seers know My origin; for I am the beginning of the gods and of the great seers in every way. Who knows Me as unborn and without beginning, the great Lord of the world — that undeluded one among mortals is released from all evils.

---

### Pratibha Commentary
Revelation continues for the delighted hearer: supreme speech spoken from welfare-wish, not from vanity. The contested claim is epistemic asymmetry — gods and ṛṣis do not know Kṛṣṇa's prabhava because He is their ādi. Knowing Him as aja and anādi, world-maheśvara, frees from all pāpa. Existentially this reorders religious authority: even celestial and seer-knowledge are downstream. Liberation-knowledge is not climbing the ladder of beings; it is recognizing the unborn source of the ladder. ### Key Terms

## Every State of Being From Me Alone
**Source:** Bhagavad Gītā 10.4–10.6

Discernment, knowledge, non-delusion, patience, truth, sense-restraint, inner quiet; pleasure and pain, being and non-being, fear and fearlessness; non-violence, equanimity, contentment, austerity, giving, fame and disgrace — the manifold states of beings arise from Me alone. The seven great seers of old and the four Manus were born of My mind; from them come these creatures of the world.

## Unshaking Yoga Through Knowing Vibhūti
**Source:** Bhagavad Gītā 10.7

Who knows in truth this manifestation-power of Mine and this yoga is joined by unshaking yoga — of this there is no doubt.

---

### Devanagari

सोऽविकम्पेन योगेन युज्यते नात्र संशयः

### Pratibha Translation
Who knows in truth this manifestation-power of Mine and this yoga is joined by unshaking yoga — of this there is no doubt.

### Pratibha Commentary
Knowledge of vibhūti and yoga is not trivia; it joins the knower in avikampa yoga — yoga without trembling. The contested claim is that metaphysical recognition stabilizes practice. Existentially, trembling yoga is often ignorance of how the Lord's powers show in the world and how His yoga holds. Knowing tattvataḥ removes the vibration of doubt that keeps joining intermittent. This verse is the hinge before the famous "I am the source" and the lists: study of excellences is meant to steady yoga, not to collect divine baseball cards. ### Key Terms
**vibhūti (विभूति)** — manifest power/excellence -> divine self-showing to be known in truth -> "superpower" misses revelatory excellence
**avikampa (अविकम्प)** — unshaking/non-trembling -> quality of yoga born of true knowing -> "confident" psychologizes yogic non-oscillation

## The Seed Without Which Nothing Stands
**Source:** Bhagavad Gītā 10.39–10.40

And whatever is the seed of all beings, that I am, O Arjuna; no being, moving or unmoving, can exist without Me. There is no end of My divine vibhūtis, O Scorcher of Foes; this has been declared as an illustration of My extent.

Whatever being shows power, fortune, or energy — understand that as born of a portion of My splendor.

---

**Source:** Bhagavad Gītā 10.42

But what is this extensive knowledge to you, O Arjuna? I support this whole universe with a single portion of Myself.

---

### Devanagari

विष्टभ्याहमिदं कृत्स्नमेकांशेन स्थितो जगत्

### IAST
athavā bahunaitena kiṃ jñātena tavārjuna|

viṣṭabhyāhamidaṃ kṛtsnamekāṃśena sthito jagat

### Pratibha Translation
But what is this extensive knowledge to you, O Arjuna? I support this whole universe with a single portion of Myself.

### Pratibha Commentary
The chapter ends by undercutting its own catalog: what need of this vistara? The universe stands on ekena aṃśena — one fragment of Me. The contested climax is simultaneous generosity and reserve: endless vibhūtis were nectar, yet all worlds are held by a portion. Existentially this births awe without exhaustion and devotion without mastery-compulsion. You have been shown peaks; now learn that the whole show is fraction. Rest in that disproportion. ### Key Terms
**aṃśa (अंश)** — portion -> the single portion supporting the universe -> "part" as spatial piece understates power-disproportion

After any long study session, close with: "the universe hangs on a portion — I do not need to finish owning the map." Bow once and stop.
